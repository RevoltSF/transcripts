Universal Awards formally invites your server to participate in our awards programme, recognising outstanding Roblox communities across Discord.

▰ 𝐂𝐀𝐓𝐄𝐆𝐎𝐑𝐘 𝐒𝐄𝐋𝐄𝐂𝐓𝐈𝐎𝐍

Participating servers may enter up to six categories. Please review the categories in <#1563854335228514305> and consider which best reflect your server's community, activities and contributions.

▰ 𝐕𝐎𝐓𝐈𝐍𝐆 & 𝐏𝐑𝐎𝐌𝐎𝐓𝐈𝐎𝐍

Following the registration period, participating servers will be included in the voting process. Each server is expected to share its nomination and voting information in a noticeable, easily accessible channel within its community and use an appropriate community ping, such as @everyone or an equivalent high-reach role, to encourage voting participation.

Crediting Universal Awards is also a requirement. All participating servers must include the official Universal Awards credit message with our server invite link in their nomination announcement. This credit must remain included even if the announcement format is customised.

▰ 𝐑𝐄𝐂𝐎𝐆𝐍𝐈𝐓𝐈𝐎𝐍

Participation provides an opportunity for your server to gain recognition, increase its visibility and connect with other communities within the Roblox community.

▰ 𝐍𝐄𝐗𝐓 𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏𝐒

To confirm your interest in participating, your server representative must:

* Join the Universal Awards server.
* Open a ticket to confirm your server’s participation.

Once your server has accepted the invitation and opened a ticket, we will provide the registration details and further information regarding the awards, announcements and voting updates.
